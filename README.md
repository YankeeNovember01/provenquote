# ProvenQuote

**Two-sided local services platform — competitor to Angie's List and Thumbtack, built to beat their weaknesses.**

---

## What We Are

ProvenQuote builds hyper-local, SEO-optimized mini-sites for every service niche in every city. We own the rankings and the leads — businesses pay to access them.

We are not a lead aggregator. We are not a marketplace. We are a traffic and lead infrastructure company that leases exclusive access to local markets.

---

## The Two Products

### ProvenQuote.com — Consumer Facing
- Visitors search for local services (landscaping, roofing, HVAC, etc.)
- Each niche × city has its own mini-site, fully optimized to rank #1 on Google
- Consumers submit quote requests or service inquiries — leads come to us
- Clean, trustworthy experience. We protect consumers by enforcing quality standards and can terminate any lessee who fails them

### ProvenQuote.ai — Business Facing (B2B)
- Businesses can **buy individual leads** from specific niches/cities
- Businesses can **lease an entire niche/city** exclusively — one business only, no sharing
- High-traffic pages may move to a **monthly bidding model**
- Businesses can view any page (leased or not) and see a CTA to buy leads or lease the slot
- Dashboard for managing their leased pages, leads, and performance

---

## The Leasing Model

**One business. One niche. One city. Exclusive.**

This is not Angie's List. We do not:
- Resell the same lead to multiple businesses
- Allow more than one business per niche/city slot
- Show, promote, or benefit any business that has not paid

When a slot is leased, the business can:
- Add their contact info, branding, photos
- Add their reviews and credentials
- Customize non-SEO elements of their page
- The page looks and functions like their own professional presence

---

## Unleased Pages — The Rules

Pages that have not been leased still rank and still collect leads. They:
- Contain high-quality, relevant niche content (guides, cost info, what to look for, FAQs)
- Do NOT mention, link to, display, or benefit any specific local business
- Do NOT show reviews, phone numbers, addresses, or GMB data of area businesses
- Convert visitors via quote request forms — leads go to our queue
- Include subtle CTAs for businesses: "Are you a [niche] professional in [city]? Get these leads."

The goal on unleased pages is to be relevant and useful to Google and AI search without helping any unpaid business rank, be found, or get leads.

---

## What We Are Not Doing

- Not reaching out to businesses with their info pre-loaded as bait
- Not favoring established businesses over new ones — anyone can lease, day one of business or day 1,000
- Not reselling leads
- Not putting 2+ businesses on the same niche/city slot
- Not helping any business before they pay

---

## Consumer Protection

If a leasing business receives complaints, fails to respond to leads, or violates quality standards, we can:
- Terminate their lease
- Remove their info from the page
- Return the slot to unleased status (leads continue flowing to our queue)

---

## Revenue Model

1. **Lead purchases** — pay per lead in specific niches/cities
2. **Niche leases** — monthly flat fee for exclusive access to a niche/city
3. **Bidding** — for high-traffic pages, monthly auctions for the lease slot
4. **Multi-city packages** — businesses can lease multiple cities

---

## Tech Stack & Infrastructure

- **provenquote.com** — Consumer-facing, SEO-optimized mini-sites per niche × city
- **provenquote.ai** — B2B dashboard for lead purchase and lease management
- **Supabase** — Core database (cities, niches, pages, leads, leases)
- **DataForSEO** — Keyword research, SERP data, GMB data (backend intel only)
- **Anthropic Claude** — Content generation for niche × city pages

---

## Data Usage Policy

GMB/business data we collect (via DataForSEO) is **backend intelligence only**:
- Helps us understand market density per niche/city
- Informs which cities/niches to prioritize for build-out
- Is NEVER displayed on pages unless that business has an active lease

Reviews: not shown on unleased pages. When a business leases, they add their own reviews and credentials.
